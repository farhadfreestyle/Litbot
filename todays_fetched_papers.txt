Title: Oscillatory Phase Coding Emerges in Adaptive RNNs for Flexible Working Memory
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06932v1
Summary: Oscillatory phase coding (OPC) is widely observed in neural systems and has been implicated in cognitive functions such as memory and sequence processing. However, its computational role and neural substrates remain largely unclear. After training, we found that OPC emerges in the RNN spontaneously: the memorized items recur at distinct phases of a shared rhythm, with oscillation amplitude encoding item identity and phase encoding serial order.
